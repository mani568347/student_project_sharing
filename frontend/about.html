Where Student Ideas
Come Together

ProjectShare is a student-driven platform for discovering, sharing, and collaborating on academic and personal projects — from first drafts to finished portfolios.

Our Mission

Every semester, thousands of students build remarkable projects — and then they sit forgotten on a hard drive. We believe knowledge should flow freely across classrooms and campuses. ProjectShare gives every student a place to showcase their work, find inspiration, and connect with peers who are building the same things.

Whether it's a capstone thesis, a hackathon entry, or a side project you're proud of — upload it, tag it, and let someone else learn from it.

What You Can Do

Upload & Share

Post your project with a title, description, category, GitHub link, tags, and an attached file (ZIP, PDF, etc.) — everything in one place.

Discover Projects

Search across titles, descriptions, and tags. Filter by category — from AI/ML to Web Dev, Mobile Apps, Data Science, and Cloud/DevOps.

Smart Tagging

Tag your projects with technologies you used (React, Python, Docker…) and find projects built with your favorite tools.

Collaborate Openly

Mark projects as open to collaboration. Reach out to other students, suggest improvements, or join forces on something bigger.

Secure & Private

JWT-authenticated sessions, BCrypt-hashed passwords, and role-based access keep your account safe. Only you can delete your projects.

Works Everywhere

Clean, responsive design built with Tailwind CSS. Use it on your laptop, tablet, or phone — no app download required.
